Hello!

I’m Soo-Hwan Chae, a Postdoctoral Associate in the General Robotics Lab at Duke University, working with Prof. Boyuan Chen. My research focuses on embodied intelligence and robotic systems that integrate adaptive AI, mechanism design, and agile locomotion for operation in unstructured environments.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Seoul National University, where I developed bio-inspired and multimodal locomotive robots with Prof. Kyu-Jin Cho. My broader research interests include small-scale robotics, smart actuation, and human–AI teaming.

You can learn more about me, explore my research, and view my publications.
You can also download my CV.
